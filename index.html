About Funmilola

A Unique Blend of
Law, Leadership & Academia

Called to the Nigerian Bar in 2003, with over two decades spanning legal practice, higher education, and human resources.

Funmilola Adetoye is a lawyer, academic, administrator, researcher, and leadership coach. She served at the National Horticultural Research Institute (NIHORT), helping establish its Legal Unit, and later joined Chrisland University as Principal Assistant Registrar and Legal Adviser.

She is passionate about helping individuals—particularly young adults—discover who they are, understand their purpose, develop the right mindset, build character and leadership capacity, and become the best version of themselves.
