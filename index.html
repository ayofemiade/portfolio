WHO IS FUNMILOLA?

A Unique Blend of
Law, Leadership & Academia

Funmilola Abeke Adetoye is a lawyer, researcher, higher education leader, ADR professional and leadership/life coach with over two decades of professional experience.

Her work sits at the intersection of law, education, leadership, research and people development.

She is passionate about helping people—particularly young adults—understand who they are, discover purpose, develop capacity and become intentional about the lives they build.

Through her professional work, research, coaching, teaching and leadership development initiatives, she seeks to help individuals and institutions navigate complexity, make better decisions and create meaningful impact.
